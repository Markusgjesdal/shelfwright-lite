# Example 1: a listing, before and after the listing-writer routine

**These are our own examples.** The product is Shelfwright Pro itself. We wrote the "before" draft the way a rushed first pass usually reads, then rewrote it by following the `listing-writer` skill: inventory first, the fill-in template, then the claims check. Nothing here is a customer's work or a sales result.

## Before (first draft)

> **THE ULTIMATE AI Skill Pack for Digital Sellers!!! [two rocket emoji]**
>
> Stop wasting time and start earning passive income with the #1 best skill pack ever made. Our 100% proven skills write perfect listings that sell. Limited time price, going up soon! Join thousands of happy sellers who love it.
>
> Works with Claude, Codex and Cursor so it's basically their official add-on. Includes tons of skills, covers, templates and more. Guaranteed to save you hours.

## Claims check on the draft (using `checklists/claims-check.md`)

| Line in the draft | Pattern hit | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| "THE ULTIMATE", "#1 best ... ever made" | Unprovable superlative | Can't be shown |
| "earning passive income" | Income claim | Describes a result we can't promise |
| "100% proven", "Guaranteed to save you hours" | Can't be backed | Nothing was measured |
| "perfect listings that sell" | Results promise | Listings do not guarantee sales |
| "Limited time price, going up soon" | Fake scarcity | The price is not changing |
| "Join thousands of happy sellers" | Invented buyer count and testimonial | There are no sales yet |
| "basically their official add-on" | Brand misuse, endorsement confusion | We are independent |
| "tons of skills, covers, templates" | Number not from inventory | The real count is 8 skills |
| Emoji and "!!!" in the title | Style | Reads as spam on a phone |

Nine problems in five lines. None of them tells a buyer what they get.

## After (from the template)

Inventory first (from the product's own spec): 8 skill folders, each a `SKILL.md` plus templates, checklists or scripts; a README; a licence; our own examples.

> **Shelfwright Pro: 8 agent skills to write, check, price and launch**
>
> *Summary:* Eight SKILL.md skills for your coding agent: write and audit the listing, QA the zip, build covers, plan prices and fees, assemble a launch packet, review sales.
>
> Launching a digital product is the same chain of jobs every time: write the listing, check the files, make a cover, pick a price, work out the launch steps, and later read the numbers. Done from memory, something gets missed.
>
> Shelfwright Pro is eight skills for your coding agent that cover that whole chain. ... The skills never publish, upload, send or pay for anything. You click the buttons.
>
> *Who it's not for:* Anyone expecting sales. These skills prepare your listing, files and plan. They do not bring buyers, and nothing here predicts income.
>
> *Works with:* agents that load SKILL.md skill folders, including Claude Code, Codex and Cursor. Shelfwright is independent and is not made, endorsed or sponsored by the makers of those tools.

The full "after" text is the description on the Shelfwright Pro product page.

## What changed and why
- **Facts from the files, not adjectives.** Every number (8 skills) comes from the inventory.
- **A "not for" section.** It sets expectations and avoids refunds from people who wanted something else.
- **Brand names only as a compatibility fact**, with an independence statement.
- **No scarcity, no testimonials, no income talk.** The listing says what the product does and stops there.
- **Open questions are marked, not guessed.** In the working draft, the refund policy was marked `[ASK USER]` until a refund window was decided, instead of being guessed.
